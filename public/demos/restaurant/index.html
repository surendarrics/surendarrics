Our kitchen

Pepper Coast cooks the food of two coasts: the tiffin rooms of Chennai on one side of India, and the toddy shops of Kerala on the other.

Spices are toasted and ground here every morning. Dosa batter ferments overnight. Fish comes in fresh and goes straight into coconut, curry leaf and tamarind.
